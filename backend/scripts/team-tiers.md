# WC 2026 — 6-tier team ranking

Reference tiering of the 48 teams qualified for FIFA World Cup 2026,
weighing FIFA ranking + recent major-tournament results (2024–2026):
EURO 2024, Copa América 2024, AFCON 2023 & 2025, Asian Cup 2023,
Gold Cup 2024/25, and WC qualifying form.

Meant as a starting point for drafts or sanity checks — not an
authoritative bracket prediction.

## Tier 1 — Elite contenders
- Argentina
- Spain
- France
- Brazil
- England
- Portugal
- Germany
- Netherlands

Reigning world champion (Argentina), EURO 2024 champion and runner-up
(Spain, England), Copa América 2024 finalist (Argentina), plus the
traditional elites. All consistently top-10 in FIFA ranking.

## Tier 2 — Strong dark horses
- Belgium
- Croatia
- Morocco
- Colombia
- Uruguay
- Switzerland
- United States
- Japan

WC 2022 semi-finalists still strong (Croatia, Morocco), Copa 2024
finalist (Colombia) and semi-finalist (Uruguay), EURO 2024 QF
(Switzerland), and the top non-European sides outside the elite tier.

## Tier 3 — Solid, can surprise
- Mexico
- Senegal
- Ivory Coast
- Turkey
- Austria
- South Korea
- Ecuador
- Australia

Gold Cup 2025 winner (Mexico), AFCON 2023 champion on home soil (Ivory
Coast), EURO 2024 QF (Turkey), Asian Cup 2023 semi-finalist (South
Korea), plus regional powers with good recent pedigree.

## Tier 4 — Middle of the pack
- Egypt
- Sweden
- Norway
- Czechia
- Scotland
- Iran
- Canada
- Paraguay

Recent EURO 2024 participants (Czechia, Scotland), finally-qualified
Scandinavian squads (Sweden, Norway with Haaland/Ødegaard), and
reliable continental qualifiers.

## Tier 5 — Outsiders
- Tunisia
- Algeria
- Ghana
- Qatar
- Saudi Arabia
- Uzbekistan
- South Africa
- Bosnia-Herzegovina

Asian Cup 2023 winner (Qatar), AFCON 2023 semi-finalist (South
Africa), World Cup regulars who rarely progress from groups, and
Uzbekistan's first-ever WC appearance.

## Tier 6 — Longest odds
- Congo DR
- Jordan
- New Zealand
- Panama
- Iraq
- Haiti
- Curaçao
- Cape Verde Islands

Asian Cup 2023 runner-up (Jordan, the surprise run), AFCON 2023
semi-finalist (Congo DR), and the WC 2026 debut minnows expanding the
field — including Curaçao, set to become the smallest nation ever at
a World Cup (~150k population), and Cape Verde making their maiden
appearance.

## Caveats

- FIFA ranking alone would push Mexico and Senegal higher than placed
  here; recent form (and the Gold Cup result for Mexico) pulled them
  into Tier 3 rather than Tier 2.
- Belgium is fading hard but squad ceiling and depth still warrant
  Tier 2.
- United States is a home-advantage bet — without the co-host factor
  they would be borderline Tier 3.
- The Tier 5/6 line is the fuzziest; Uzbekistan and Bosnia could
  reasonably swap with Jordan or Congo DR.
